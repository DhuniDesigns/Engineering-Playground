Sello Interaction Lab

Five micro-interactions from Sello, one for each weekday starting Monday 26 October. Each stage loops on its own. Open it in Focus, screen-record one or two loops, then paste the caption.

RecordPress Focus, then Win+Shift+R on Windows or Cmd+Shift+5 on Mac and drag over the stage.
LengthEach loop runs 7 to 10 seconds. One clean loop is enough for X; two works well on LinkedIn.
FormatThe stage is 4:5 portrait, which takes up the most space in both the X and LinkedIn feeds.
