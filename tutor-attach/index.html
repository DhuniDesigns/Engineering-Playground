I’m nervous about listing calls. What do I say when a seller asks, ‘Can you lower your commission?

Totally normal. Use a calm bridge, then value. Try this:

“I’m focused on netting you the most, not charging the least. Can I show how our pricing, prep, and marketing usually put more back in your pocket—even after commission?”
